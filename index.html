Years that pass for the traveler at each speed. Drag across the chart to change the speed. The curve stays nearly flat at everyday speeds, then plunges as you approach light speed.

Add number of years in first field, add in a percentage speed of light in the second, and get a result that is the number of years that would pass for the person going that speed.
